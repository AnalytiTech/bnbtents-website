Privacy policy

Your privacy

Last updated October 16, 2026.

BNB Tents (Horseheads, NY) runs this website and the BNB Tents Quotes & Invoices app. This page explains what information each one handles.

Quotes & Invoices app

The app is a tool BNB Tents uses to write quotes and invoices for its customers.

  • Everything entered in the app (customer names, phone numbers, emails, event details, prices) is stored only on the device it was entered on.
  • The app has no account, no server and no analytics. It does not collect, upload, sell or share any data.
  • A PDF leaves the device only when the user chooses to send, save or print it, using the phone's own share menu.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data from the device.

Website

  • When you request a quote, the details you type in the form (name, phone, email, event details) are emailed to BNB Tents so we can reply. Form messages may pass through a form-delivery service that forwards them to our inbox.
  • We use that information only to answer your request and plan your event. We never sell it.
  • The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages; both may record standard technical data such as IP addresses.
